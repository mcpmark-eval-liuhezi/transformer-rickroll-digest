## Part 1: The Paper — "Attention Is All You Need"

- **Full title:** Attention Is All You Need
- **arXiv identifier:** arXiv:1706.03762 (current version v7; submitted 12 Jun 2017, last revised 2 Aug 2023)
- **First author:** Ashish Vaswani (with Noam Shazeer, Niki Parmar, Jakob Uszkoreit, Llion Jones, Aidan N. Gomez, Lukasz Kaiser, and Illia Polosukhin)
- **Year:** 2017
- **Subjects:** Computation and Language (cs.CL); Machine Learning (cs.LG)
- **arXiv link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762

### One-sentence takeaway from the abstract

The paper proposes the **Transformer**, "a new simple network architecture… based solely on attention mechanisms, dispensing with recurrence and convolutions entirely," which on two WMT 2014 machine translation tasks achieved superior quality while being more parallelizable and requiring significantly less time to train (28.4 BLEU on WMT 2014 English-to-German and a new single-model state-of-the-art 41.8 BLEU on WMT 2014 English-to-French).

### Quote worth printing (from the arXiv abstract page)

> "We propose a new simple network architecture, the Transformer, based solely on attention mechanisms, dispensing with recurrence and convolutions entirely."
