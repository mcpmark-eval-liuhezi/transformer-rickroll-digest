# Memes & Machines — Weekly Digest

This week's edition pairs one classic internet artifact with one landmark AI paper.

## The Paper: Attention Is All You Need

- **Title:** Attention Is All You Need
- **arXiv identifier:** [arXiv:1706.03762](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)
- **First author:** Ashish Vaswani (with Noam Shazeer, Niki Parmar, Jakob Uszkoreit, Llion Jones, Aidan N. Gomez, Lukasz Kaiser, and Illia Polosukhin)
- **One-sentence takeaway (from the abstract):** The paper proposes "a new simple network architecture, the Transformer, based solely on attention mechanisms, dispensing with recurrence and convolutions entirely" — the design that still underpins today's large language models.

## The Video: Never Gonna Give You Up

- **Exact title:** Rick Astley - Never Gonna Give You Up (Official Video) (4K Remaster)
- **Quoted lyric:** "Never gonna give you up."

## Why the Pairing Fits

Both are 2017 artifacts with the same promise: the Transformer attends to every part of a sequence without letting anything down, and Rick Astley vows to never give you up, never let you down, never run around and desert you.
